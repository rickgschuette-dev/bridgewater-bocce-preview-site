Mandatory Survey For All Fall League Registrants

We require every player interested in playing this upcoming Fall Season to complete our player survey as soon as possible. If you are not interested, we also need to hear back from you as soon as possible.

Our Fall Bridgewater Recreational Bocce League season will begin during the first week of October 2026 and finish in December. We have many new players interested in joining. Traditionally, the Fall League has been scheduled on Mondays at 4:30 p.m. Last spring, we moved the start time to 4:00 p.m. to accommodate additional players and teams. To include an even larger group of new players this fall, we are considering moving the Monday start time to 3:00 p.m. In addition, we may need to add another day. If we do, teams will rotate different weeks with different days, still maintaining 1 day per week per team. In the spirit of keeping the social atmosphere we have all worked hard to develop and maintain, we will not break up into separate leagues on different days. We will rotate, play with all our friends, and have fun doing it.

We are wrapping up a very successful Summer League, played on Saturdays at 9:30 a.m. Instead of 4 players per team, we used 6 players per team, with each team playing 3 games instead of 2 per week — everyone played at least 2 games per team. We developed rules that allowed as few as 3 players per team without a forfeit, resulting in no outside subs and more flexibility with scheduling conflicts. This is the format we intend to use going forward for the Fall season.

We need a separate response from each player.
