Clinics & Classes

The Bridgewater Bocce Club is an officially recognized HOA club operated under terms and conditions set forth by the Bridgewater at Viera Board of Directors. Play and participation in all club events is open to all residents. Bocce courts are open and available to all residents for open play during Amenity Center hours.

Participation in Bridgewater Bocce Club activities, events, and open play is voluntary. Residents participate at their own risk and are responsible for their own safety and well-being while on the courts.
